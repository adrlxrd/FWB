THE STANDARD

FWB started with a very simple problem.

One day, Suhani and her co-founder Palak were walking together, casually cribbing about how difficult it was to find the right people to build with. People with ambition were everywhere but finding people with ambition, skills, consistency and the willingness to actually execute was much harder.

That conversation sparked FWB. Today, we’re building a curated ecosystem where founders, creators, developers, designers, marketers and other ambitious individuals can find people worth building with.

Every member is manually reviewed because our goal was never to create the biggest community. It’s to create the right one.
